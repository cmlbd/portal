Our Mission

We equip every citizen with the tools to navigate the complex media landscape critically and responsibly.

01
🔍

Critical Analysis

Developing skills to evaluate media messages, identify bias, and distinguish fact from opinion across all platforms.

🛡️

About CML

Who We Are

The Centre for Media Literacy is a non-profit organisation committed to building a society that can think critically about the media it consumes and creates. Founded by media professionals, educators, and civil society leaders, CML operates across Bangladesh.

Our multidisciplinary team of advisors, trainers, and researchers works tirelessly to equip citizens — from students to journalists — with the skills needed in a rapidly changing information landscape.
